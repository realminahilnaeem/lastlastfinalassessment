Eligibility Criteria

Applicant Must

  • Domicile From Any District Of The Punjab
  • Passed Intermediate/Equivalent Examination
  • The Candidate Must Not Be Older Than 21 Years Of Age
  • Students Currently Intermediate / A-Level In The Last Calendar Year
  • A Placement In A University Listed In The Top 100 QS World General Ranking And Top 100 In QS Subject-Wise Ranking

Applicants Not Eligible

The Candidates Who Is Already Availing Any Other International Scholarship Or Study Abroad
